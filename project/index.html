Blog approach

Three original Dutch review drafts are prepared. Publish only after medical review under an approved author, with relevant references, a real review date and internal links. Future topics should come from actual Search Console questions and consultation needs. Prefer useful answers over bulk SEO pages; avoid duplicate doorway pages and invented expertise.

Technical SEO and AI-search approach

Retain existing useful URLs; audit redirects only where URLs change. Use one clear H1, descriptive headings, crawlable internal links, unique metadata, correct self-canonicals and an XML sitemap on the confirmed domain. Match structured data to visible approved content and avoid duplicate SEO-plugin schema. Check status codes, accessibility, image dimensions, loading and field performance. Preview stays noindex. AI visibility follows the same strong content and technical foundations; no special markup or guaranteed citations are promised.

References: Google AI search guidance · Google personalised advertising policy.

SEA readiness — no campaigns activated

  • Prepare search-intent groups for Hairfiller consultation/prices, treatment zones, and Hairfiller versus REYU. Confirm language and geographic service areas with the clinic before implementation.
  • Use the relevant treatment, price or comparison section as the landing target. Keep claims factual, include suitability/risks and a clear consultation CTA. Do not promise regrowth or use misleading before/after ads.
  • Provisional negatives to review: jobs, training/course, wholesale, DIY injections and unrelated free products. Do not exclude useful informational queries without search-term evidence.
  • Before launch: approved account/budget/locations, medical advertising policy review, consent/CMP validation, actual booking-completion attribution, mobile landing QA and approved clinical copy.
  • Do not build health-condition remarketing audiences or upload patient lists. Do not treat consultation clicks as completed bookings. No campaigns, spend or vendor tags have been activated.

Clinical and media approval register

  • Source efficacy figures (22.3% density / 26% thickness), first-session hair-loss cessation and one-year duration need underlying evidence, population, timeframe and clinician approval.
  • Claims about new hair follicles, prevention and transplant survival are held for review rather than republished as established effects.
  • Source protocols conflict: four sessions every two weeks versus three–four sessions every two–four weeks; session duration appears as both fifteen and thirty minutes. Confirm standard, intensive and maintenance protocols.
  • Approve contraindications, risks, aftercare, REYU comparison and all three draft articles against the current consent document. No personal medical advice or guaranteed results.
  • Approve reuse of the original zone diagram, all patient photos, video/testimonial material and reviews on the separate mini-site. Source provenance does not establish patient reuse consent.

  • Assess AI-search referrals/citations where observable; no guaranteed AI placement or invented GEO score
