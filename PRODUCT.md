# Sarasin Hairfiller review site

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People of all genders researching hair loss, clinic treatments, prices, preparation, aftercare and appropriate next steps. Mobile browsing is a client priority.

## Product Purpose

An independent, review-only Hairfiller mini-site that makes the existing clinic information easier to read and supports an informed personal consultation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all treatment-page reader content, tables, imagery, links, videos, reviews and author information. Do not silently substitute summaries for original wording.
- Existing static HTML/CSS/JavaScript implementation and Vercel preview. No main clinic WordPress or Shopify mutations.
- Clinical claims, treatment protocols, image/media reuse and production integration remain subject to authorised approval. The review deployment stays noindex.
- Native disclosures, keyboard-accessible tabs, overlay menu, manual/pauseable image carousel and click-to-load external videos.

## Brand Commitments

Sarasin's name, Dutch content, green/ivory palette, Bricolage Grotesque typography, rounded sections, real clinical evidence and Skin Longevity 360° context. The user supplied desktop/mobile PDF exports as the new composition reference, not as a source of another brand's claims.

## Evidence on Hand

`audit/source-complete-2026-11-10.json` captures original HTML, text, image metadata, links and media. `content/homepage-source.json` contains lossless semantic content groups. Clinical before/after images remain original. Generated lifestyle assets are illustrative and must be labelled, never presented as patient outcomes.

## Product Principles

- Preserve source truth and show uncertainty transparently.
- Prioritise readable, accessible mobile content.
- Keep the next action clear without fabricated reviews, guarantees or treatment outcomes.
- Separate a verified review preview from an approved production launch.
