# Hairfiller review QA — 8 November 2026

## Scope and evidence

- Source audit: 139 captured paragraph/list/table blocks, including seven original tables. `tests/content-parity.cjs` compares exact source text/table cells and verifies their review-archive destinations.
- Main journey: visible general-clinic reviews, two patient-story video controls, two press links, original zone diagram, complete author biography and substantive treatment tables. Original clinical claims remain attributed review copy pending approval.
- Responsive checks: expanded content at 320, 393, 768 and 1440 px without horizontal page overflow or clipped table cells. Existing client-feedback suite covers nine viewport widths, overlay navigation, keyboard controls and video cleanup.
- Pilot regression suite: six routes / 36 route-viewport combinations, 40 original gallery images, 14 FAQ topics, nine unique video IDs, consent defaults/revocation and resource checks.
- Editable Figma: dated current desktop/mobile frames, linked full-table/FAQ/gallery/source views; rendered samples inspected and visible text-box bounds checked. See `audit/figma-verification-2026-11-08.json`.
- Build repetition produced identical generated page output. JavaScript syntax, XML parsing, whitespace and handoff ZIP integrity checked.

## Limits

These are browser-emulation and content checks, not physical-device tests, clinical approval or proof of search/conversion performance. The visual detector also reports heuristic spacing/heading warnings, including table/checklist wrappers; it is not an all-clear accessibility certification. WordPress package is an unactivated staging handoff, not an installed or runtime-tested production integration. Main clinic WordPress and Shopify were not changed.

Release is accepted only after the same three browser suites pass against the published review URL; their command output is the evidence, not this checklist alone.
