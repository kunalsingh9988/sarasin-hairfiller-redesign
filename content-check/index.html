3. What exactly do hair fillers do?

Hair fillers are the ultimate treatment for hair loss, and provide fuller and thicker hair.

CONTENT-038 · p — Hair fillers are a method to address thinning hair by adding extra volume and texture to the hair roots. This is achieved by injecting a filler, usually based on hyaluronic acid, into the scalp. The hyaluronic acid promotes the growth of new hair and also strengthens existing hair, making it look fuller and healthier.

Frequently asked questions about hair filler

What can I expect from a hair filler treatment?

CONTENT-149 · p — Our doctor or nurse will analyze your scalp and hair before the treatment and create a treatment plan with you to help achieve the desired result. Clinical research shows that hair filler treatment yields visibly good results with 4 sessions at a 2-week interval. We are happy to advise you on the required quantity and treatment frequency. During the treatment, our doctor or nurse will administer multiple injections using a very fine needle into the chosen zone(s) on your scalp. Additionally, clients are recommended to use our hair serum daily during and after the Hair Filler treatment to maintain the result in the long term.

What are the risks and complications of a hair filler?

CONTENT-151 · p — A hair filler treatment at Sarasin Clinic is a very safe procedure. Complications are limited to some redness and mild skin irritation immediately after injection. These often disappear within 72 hours.

View on the homepage

What is the result of a hair filler?

CONTENT-152 · p — The hair loss process stops after just the first treatment session with Hairfiller. Subsequent sessions stimulate blood circulation and significantly revitalize the hair follicles, thereby accelerating hair growth. Laboratory research has shown that peptide therapy revitalizes hair cells and hair follicles after treatment and stimulates blood circulation and hydration in the scalp. During clinical research into its effectiveness, patients were observed to experience an average increase in hair density of 22.3% and an average increase in hair thickness of 26% after one session every two weeks for a period of eight weeks (four sessions). A full Hair Filler treatment at Sarasin Clinic could counteract the hair loss process for up to approximately one year.

How many hair filler sessions are needed on average?

CONTENT-160 · p — A hair filler treatment is always performed as a course. The minimum is 4 sessions, usually with an interval of 2 to 4 weeks. This is necessary because the active peptides and hyaluronic acid build up their effect step by step in the scalp and around the hair follicles. After the initial course, a maintenance session every few months can help maintain the result.
