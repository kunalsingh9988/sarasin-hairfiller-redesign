## Final prompt set

The following template was submitted separately for each image, substituting the zone and description listed below:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: standalone website treatment-zone photograph for a hair clinic price card, {zone} zone. Generate ONE high-resolution landscape photograph, 8:5 aspect ratio. Close overhead view of an adult person's scalp and crown showing {description}. Authentic photographic human hair strands, realistic skin pores, subtle natural imperfections, believable anatomy; professional clinical documentary photography, soft diffuse daylight, neutral warm skin and dark brown hair against a softly blurred pale sage clinic backdrop. Frame the crown centrally with the entire relevant scalp area clearly visible, no face, no identifiable features. Gender-neutral presentation. This is a standalone illustrative scalp photo, NOT a before/after, NOT a treatment result. No text, labels, arrows, circles, vector overlays, diagram, split screen, collage, watermark, hands, syringes, wounds, dramatic lighting or glossy 3D render. Natural realistic photographic image; match a clean premium clinic website.

- Small: a small localized subtly thinning patch near the crown, dense hair around it.
- Medium: a medium-sized area of visibly thinning hair around the crown with scalp visible between remaining hairs.
- Large: a larger area of diffuse thinning across the top and crown with natural sparse remaining hair.
